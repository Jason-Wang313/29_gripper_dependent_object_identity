# Submission Readiness Decision

Decision: workshop-only / strong-revise.

## Why Not Submit-Ready

- Evidence is a hand-specified finite toy world.
- No real robot, tactile, force, or high-fidelity simulation validation is included.
- The theorem is an abstraction result, not a prevalence result.
- V2 shows ICIP fails when the task needs an unobservable attribute.
- There is no active sensing, multimodal fusion, or abstention policy.

## Why Not Kill

- The identity-as-quotient mechanism is crisp and distinct from fixed-label recognition.
- The non-nested partition condition is formal and easy to audit.
- The main experiment separates global ID, common label, and ICIP behavior cleanly.
- The v2 stress honestly narrows the claim to observable task-relevant quotients.

## Required Next Work

- Build a real or high-fidelity multi-gripper dataset.
- Estimate observation and task partitions empirically.
- Add active sensing/abstention for hidden task attributes.
- Compare against universal latent-state and multimodal object-representation baselines.
